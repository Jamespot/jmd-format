Memo to the Northwind Ops leadership team — Q3 review and the plan for Q4

Where we stand. We closed Q3 with 212 customers, up from 188 at the end of Q2. The installed base reached 260,000 users. ARR is €5.1M, growing 14% year on year. Net revenue retention stayed at 108%. Support handled 3,900 tickets with a median first answer of 41 minutes.

What changed this quarter. We shipped the new incident board in July, the mobile app in August, and the directory sync in September. The board is now used by 71% of active workspaces. The mobile app has 18,000 installs. Directory sync is live at 34 customers, all in the public sector.

Two ways to grow in Q4. Option A, land: 40 new mid-size customers through partners, with a shorter onboarding (target 9 days instead of 16). Option B, expand: move 25 existing customers from the crisis cell to the whole organisation, which triples seats per account. We recommend B first, A second: expansion costs a third of a new sale and our churn is lowest where the whole organisation is on the platform.

The plan, in order. October: hire two customer success managers and publish the expansion playbook. November: run the first 10 expansions with the current team and measure seats and time to value. December: decide on partners for A based on the November numbers, and prepare the January release.

Risks. The public-sector procurement calendar slips every year in December. Two engineers are out in November. The security audit for ISO 27001 renewal is scheduled for 12 November and needs three people for a week.

What we ask of you. Approve the two hires this month. Confirm that expansion comes before new logos in the sales compensation for Q4. Agree that the January release is frozen on 15 December.
